First-time setup (2 minutes, once):
1. Install Ollama from ollama.com and launch it.
2. Pull a brain in a terminal: ollama pull qwen3:8b  ·  smaller/faster: qwen3:1.7b  ·  stronger GPU: qwen3:32b
3. Press Refresh — your downloaded brains appear above.
After the download, everything runs offline on your machine. If this page can't reach Ollama, open it through http://localhost:8000 (any static server) instead of double-clicking the file.
How it works: the first time you initialize, your chosen model (a few hundred MB to a few GB) downloads from a public CDN and is cached in your browser. Every visit after that loads instantly and runs fully offline — try airplane mode. Bigger brains know more but need a stronger GPU; start with the recommended one. Engine: @mlc-ai/web-llm over WebGPU.
